Goal Math Explained

The system dynamically computes the required monthly contribution based on the months remaining until your target date:

Remaining = Target - Current
Monthly Req = Remaining / Months Left

This required monthly contribution is automatically factored into your Available Investment Capacity so you never overcommit funds needed for critical goals.

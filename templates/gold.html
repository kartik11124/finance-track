Gold in Personal Asset Allocation

Gold acts as an essential hedge against inflation and macroeconomic uncertainty. In the Indian financial ecosystem, gold can be held through:

  • Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB): Backed by the RBI, offers 2.5% p.a. regular interest plus tax-free capital appreciation upon maturity.
  • Gold ETFs & Mutual Funds: Dematerialized high-liquidity units traded on the NSE/BSE tracking 99.5% pure physical gold.
  • Digital Gold: Small fractional ownership accessible with entry tickets as low as ₹100.

GoldAPI Configuration

Gold price data is retrieved via https://www.goldapi.io/api/price/XAU/INR.

API Key: Loaded from GOLD_API_KEY in root .env
Base Currency: Indian Rupee (INR)
Unit Standard: Gram & Troy Ounce
Persistence: Cached in SQLite table gold_data

If GOLD_API_KEY is empty or invalid, the platform gracefully delivers the latest cached rate and alerts the user without breaking execution.
